🖨️ Task 1 — Digitise Your Prints

Bring your prints into Photoshop.

Scan

  1. 300 dpi or higher
  2. Colour mode (even for black)
  3. Print lies flat
  4. Save as JPG, PNG or TIFF

No scanner? Phone photo · from above · good light · no shadows.

Set Up

  1. New document: A3 landscape · 300 dpi
  2. File → Place Embedded
  3. Duplicate the layer Cmd/Ctrl+J
  4. Save as PSD
Keep your original. Work on the copy.

⚫ Task 2 — Threshold & Remove White

Make your print black and white. Remove the white background.

1
Apply Threshold
▶
  1. Image → Adjustments → Threshold
  2. Move the slider
  3. Click OK
2
Select the White
▶
  1. Rectangular Marquee M
  2. Draw a box in a white area
  3. Select → Similar
3
Delete the White
▶
  1. Press Delete
  2. Only the texture is left
  3. Deselect Cmd/Ctrl+D

Also: Magic Wand (W)

Areas of one colour

  • Tolerance 20–40
  • Shift add · Alt/Opt remove

Also: Polygonal Lasso (L)

Straight edges

  • Click points around the shape
  • Join back to the start

🎭 Task 3 — Masks, Blend Modes & Colour

Control your texture. Mix it into the page. Add colour.

1
Layer Mask
▶
  1. Layers panel → Add Layer Mask
  2. Black brush = hide
  3. White brush = show again
2
Blend Modes
▶

Layers panel → change Normal to:

MultiplyDarker · best on light backgrounds
OverlayMore contrast
ScreenLighter
Soft LightGentle contrast

Use Opacity to make it softer.

3
Add Colour
▶
  1. Image → Adjustments → Hue/Saturation
  2. Tick Colorize
  3. Move the sliders

💡 Quick colour

  • Put a colour fill layer underneath
  • Set the texture to a blend mode

Task 3 Checklist ✓

Added a layer mask · hid areas with black
Tried 2+ blend modes · changed opacity
Added colour
Screenshot taken
🧵 Task 4 — Use It in Your Project

Put your texture into your Term 2 design.

Backgrounds

Depth behind figures

Surfaces

On fabric or garments

Collage

Layered design sheets

Details

Small accents

1
Copy
▶
  • Drag the layer into your project file
  • or Cmd/Ctrl+C → Cmd/Ctrl+V
2
Position & Scale
▶
  • Cmd/Ctrl+T
  • Drag corners (Shift keeps the shape)
  • Enter to confirm
3
Refine
▶
  • Blend mode
  • Layer mask
  • Lower opacity
  • Match your project colours
